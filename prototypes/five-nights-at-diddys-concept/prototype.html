How to survive

  • Escape: find your stuff (phone, wallet, car keys…), then get out the front door in the Grand Foyer.
  • Or outlast him: survive until 6 AM and the party's over.
  • Baby oil: drop a puddle behind you. Diddy slips on it and goes down for a few seconds.
  • Flashlight and CCTV share one battery. Spare batteries are lying around.
  • He hears you sprint, and he spots your flashlight from further away.
  • Grab the last item and he knows. Run for the door.
