The game plays sideways: turn your phone once it starts. Left thumb moves, drag on the right to look, and the buttons on the right strike, shield and throw. SKIP passes over a scene.

The first start takes up to half a minute while the city is built. Voices and music begin when you press Play; on an iPhone, turn the volume up. The picture sharpens or softens by itself to keep the game smooth, and Options has a Graphics setting.
