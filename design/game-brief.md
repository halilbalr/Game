# Game Brief: Blockheim

**One-sentence pitch:** A pocket-sized, play-in-the-browser block world where you punch trees, craft tools, dig for diamonds and survive the night - built to feel like the classic first-person voxel sandbox on a phone.

## Core loop
- Gather blocks and drops (wood, stone, ores, food) by mining with the right tool.
- Craft better tools, a furnace, torches and a shelter from those materials (recipe book on touch).
- Use the new tools to go deeper (iron, gold, diamond) and survive harder nights (zombies, Blastlings).
- Build freely; in Creative mode skip survival and just build and fly.

## Player goal & fail state — what "working" looks like
- A session feels complete when the player has gone from bare hands to an iron pickaxe and a lit shelter, survived a night, and the world is still there when they return.
- Fail state: health reaches zero (falls, drowning, lava, starvation, mobs, explosions) - the inventory drops where you died and you respawn at your bed or the world spawn.

## MVP — what must exist to be the game
- Infinite seeded terrain with biomes, caves, ores, water and trees; day/night cycle with sky light and torch light.
- Mining with hardness and tool tiers, block placement, item drops and pickup.
- Inventory, 2x2 and 3x3 crafting (shaped and shapeless recipes), furnace smelting, chests.
- Survival stats: health, hunger, breath, fall damage; food.
- Mobs: passive animals and hostile night mobs with combat.
- Touch controls (joystick, drag-look, tap/hold) plus keyboard and mouse.
- Local saves of multiple worlds; Survival and Creative modes.

## Out of scope — not building this
- Multiplayer, chat, commands, the Nether/End dimensions, redstone, enchanting, villages, boats, minecarts.
- Any asset, name or character taken from Mojang's game: every texture, sprite, mob design and sound is generated by this project's code, and hostile mob designs are original (the Blastling is not a reskin).
- App-store packaging (the game ships as an installable web app / PWA).

## Build order
1. Voxel engine core: chunk storage, seeded terrain, flood-fill lighting, meshing (prove it runs on a phone).
2. Player movement, collision, raycast, break/place.
3. Inventory, crafting, tools, drops.
4. Survival stats, mobs, day/night danger.
5. Touch UI polish, saves, audio, PWA build.

---
**Who it's for / what they feel:** Players who love block sandboxes and want the same "one more block" feeling on their phone, in Swedish or English.

**Art & audio direction:** 16x16 pixel textures with procedural noise, blocky low-poly mobs, synthesized crunchy block sounds and a sparse generative piano ambience.

**Reference game:** Minecraft (classic Survival/Creative) - the survival loop, block set, crafting and controls; this MVP keeps the overworld only.
